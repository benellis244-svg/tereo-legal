What is free and what is paid

Tereo is free to download. The free tier includes the daily verse, the lesson stages, and a selection of the Bible — Psalms, John and Romans — at no cost.

On the free tier, a wrong answer costs a heart. You start with five, and one returns every four hours. When you run out you can wait for them to refill, or subscribe.

Tereo Pro is a paid subscription. It adds:

  • Pathway mode — every unit on all three paths
  • Leaderboards, with friends and globally
  • Unlimited hearts, so a wrong answer never stops you
  • Rapid Recall — hard-mode review sessions
  • Read & Highlight — full chapters you can mark up
  • Two additional streak freezes each month

The features included in the free and paid tiers may change over time. If a feature you already paid for is removed during a subscription period, you may cancel and request a refund through the store you bought it from.
